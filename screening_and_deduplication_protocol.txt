Article Screening and Deduplication Protocol

Research Project: Media Frames of China's Poverty Alleviation: The Case of The New York Times

===========================================
SEARCH PARAMETERS
===========================================

Database: LexisNexis
Source: The New York Times
Period: November 3, 2013 - December 31, 2023
Search Strategy: See Appendix 1 for complete search terms


===========================================
INCLUSION CRITERIA
===========================================

✓ News articles with substantive discussion of China's poverty alleviation policies, programs, or outcomes
✓ Published within the specified time period


===========================================
EXCLUSION CRITERIA
===========================================

✗ Brief or passing mentions without substantive discussion
✗ Wire service content (AP, Reuters, etc.)
✗ Duplicate or republished articles
✗ Letters to the editor and reader comments
✗ Opinion pieces and editorials


===========================================
SCREENING PROCESS
===========================================

Two independent reviewers screened all retrieved articles using the above criteria.

Inter-rater reliability: Cohen's kappa = 0.94

Discrepancies were resolved through discussion until consensus was reached.

Final corpus: 254 unique news articles (384,553 tokens)


===========================================
DATA PROCESSING
===========================================

- Articles exported from LexisNexis in Word format
- Converted to plain text (.txt) files
- Organized by publication year (2013-2023)
- Each file named by article headline
